SECTION 03 // OPTICAL CALIBRATION NOTES

Best Practices for Accurate Lens & Mask Detection

  • 01. Center the Facial Zone: Keep eyewear frames and lower-face mask pleats inside the optical viewfinder reticle without cropping the bridge or ear loops.
  • 02. Minimize Lens Glare: Avoid harsh direct reflections across tinted sunglasses lenses so frame rims and temple contours remain crisp.
  • 03. Verify Confidence Tiers: Scores at 80% or higher indicate a High Clarity Match; lower values prompt adjusting angle or lighting.
